Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 28, 2026

The NatCA QoE App ("the App") is operated by the National Telecommunications Commission of Sierra Leone (NatCA). This policy explains what data the App collects, why it is collected, and how it is handled.

1. Data We Collect

Data typePurposeWhen collected
Location (GPS coordinates) Geo-tag network quality complaints so regulators can identify coverage gaps When you submit a complaint
Device & network info (model, OS version, carrier, signal strength, cell ID, radio type) Measure network quality of service (QoS) and attach diagnostics to complaints When you submit a complaint or run a network test
Camera & photos (ID document images, selfie) Know Your Customer (KYC) identity verification required for complaint submission During KYC registration only
Account information (name, email, phone number) Create your account and communicate complaint status updates During registration
Biometric authentication Secure app login via fingerprint or face unlock (processed on-device only) At login, if enabled

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Process and investigate your network quality complaints
  • Generate aggregate QoS statistics for regulatory oversight
  • Verify your identity through KYC to prevent fraudulent complaints
  • Communicate with you about complaint status

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secured servers and transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. KYC documents are stored securely and accessible only to authorized NatCA administrators for identity verification purposes. Biometric data (fingerprint/face) is processed entirely on your device and never transmitted to our servers.

4. Data Retention

Account and complaint data is retained for the duration of your account plus 2 years for regulatory record-keeping. KYC documents are retained as required by Sierra Leone telecommunications regulations. You may request deletion of your account by contacting us.

5. Your Rights

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Withdrawal: Revoke permissions (location, camera) at any time through your device settings

6. Permissions

The App requests the following device permissions, each of which you can grant or deny:

  • Camera: Capture ID documents and selfie for KYC verification
  • Location: Geo-tag complaints for coverage analysis
  • Phone state: Read carrier and cell tower information for network diagnostics
  • Photos/media: Select existing photos for KYC document upload
  • Biometrics: Optional secure login

The App functions with reduced features if permissions are denied (e.g., complaints without location data will not include geographic information).

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. KYC verification requires a valid government-issued ID, which inherently limits use to adults.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the App and on this page with a revised effective date.
